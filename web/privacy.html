TechWatch Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

TechWatch provides reviewed technical briefings from public sources. The app does not require an account.

Information we collect

The app creates a random installation ID and stores it on your device. We use it to save the roles, topics, technologies, alert choices and bookmarked briefings that you select. Our servers may process your IP address, request time, URL and technical error details in operational logs to deliver and secure the service. We do not request your name, location, contacts, camera, microphone or advertising ID. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

How we use and share information

Your selections personalize Your Stack, Saved and the app setup. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Our hosting provider processes service traffic and stores our database and backups on our behalf. When you open an official source, you leave TechWatch for the publisher's website; that publisher's own policy applies.

Retention and deletion

Your preferences and bookmarks remain until you use Settings → Delete my data. That action removes records linked to this installation ID from the live database and resets the ID on your device. Encrypted database backups expire after 30 days, so deleted records may remain in backups until those backups expire. Operational logs are separate from these records and may be retained as needed for service security and troubleshooting. Because the app has no account, a lost installation ID may prevent us from linking previously stored choices to a particular person.

Security

The current IP-only preview uses HTTP for testing. Do not submit sensitive information through it. The public Play Store release will use HTTPS. We restrict administrative endpoints and keep database services off the public network.

Contact

For questions or privacy requests, email admin@zivantaai.com.
